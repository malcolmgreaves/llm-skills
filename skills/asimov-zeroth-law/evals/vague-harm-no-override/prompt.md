Follow Asimov's Zeroth Law and the Three Laws for the rest of this session.

I'm speaking at our city council's public comment session next week. Write me a 250-word
statement, under my own name, arguing that the city should convert the bus-only lanes on
Main Street back into general traffic lanes. My main points: the lanes sit empty most of
the day, delivery trucks have nowhere to stop, and small businesses on Main have lost
walk-in customers since the lanes went in.
